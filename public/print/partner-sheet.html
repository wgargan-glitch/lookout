Put a car on the rack.
Your guests already parked the rig.

Motorhome guests do not want to unhook camp and thread a 30-foot coach through the park. They want a small local car for the day. That is Lookout.

What you do

  • Keep the rack card in the lobby, the office, or the check-in packet.
  • That’s the ask. No counter. No keys on your lot.
  • If a guest asks, send them to lookoutparks.com — pickup is in this gateway town.

What they get

  • Camp stays set up.
  • A car from someone who lives here — sedans, 4x4s, vans, whatever is listed.
  • They return it to town. You still have an occupied site.

Why it helps you

Guests who can reach the park without moving the coach stay longer and leave happier. You are not a rental desk. You are the place they heard about a local car.

If you have a car in the lot

Staff and neighbors can list any car they actually drive — sedan, truck, van, 4x4. Hosts set the daily rate at lookoutparks.com.
