# Lookout EDDM — dual purpose, 6.5 × 9 in

USPS Every Door Direct Mail **Retail** postcard for gateway towns.

Two audiences on one card:

1. **RV / motorhome guests** — leave camp set up, rent a local car to see the park.
2. **Hosts** — list **any** car (sedan, van, truck, 4x4). Not 4x4s only.

## Size

| | |
|---|---|
| Trim | **6.5 in high × 9 in wide** |
| Why it qualifies | Height is over the 6.125 in flat minimum |
| Stock | 14 pt or 16 pt C2S, color both sides |
| File | `lookout-eddm-6.5x9.pdf` (page 1 front, page 2 **address side**) at 300 DPI |

A 4 × 6 or 6 × 9 postcard will be rejected at the counter.

## Address side (USPS, not optional)

EDDM Retail is a **flat**. The mailing label must sit in the **top 3.25 in**. Retail does **not** use a permit number.

Printed on the back:

- Return address, upper left (fill street + ZIP before print)
- Five-line Retail indicia, upper right:

      PRSRT STD
      ECRWSS
      U.S. POSTAGE
      PAID
      EDDM RETAIL

- `LOCAL POSTAL CUSTOMER` under the indicia (still in the top half)

Do not put a city or permit number in the indicia. That is the BMEU / permit product.

Retail saturates **every** active delivery on the route (homes and businesses). Homes-only requires a BMEU permit.

## Before you print

1. Replace `[Street]` and `[City ST  ZIP]` with a real domestic return address.
2. Add the live URL / QR once the domain is chosen.
3. Add **0.125 in bleed** if the printer asks.
4. Do not put photos or reversed type in the indicia or “Local Postal Customer” block.

## At the Post Office

- [USPS EDDM Retail](https://www.usps.com/business/every-door-direct-mail.htm), [EDDM map](https://eddm.usps.com/eddm/), Form 5110.
- Retail cap: 5,000 pieces per day, per 5-digit ZIP, dropped at that ZIP’s Post Office.
- Bundles of 50–100 with facing slips, one route per bundle.
