# Lookout EDDM mailer — 6.5 × 9 in

USPS Every Door Direct Mail Retail postcard. Landscape, two-sided.

## Size (do not crop)

| | |
|---|---|
| Trim | **6.5 in high × 9 in wide** |
| Why it qualifies | Height 6.5 in is over the 6.125 in flat minimum |
| Stock | 14 pt or 16 pt C2S, full color both sides |
| File | `lookout-eddm-6.5x9.pdf` (page 1 front, page 2 back) at 300 DPI |

A 4 × 6 or 6 × 9 postcard will be rejected at the counter.

## Before you print

1. In Canva or Acrobat, replace `[CITY, ST]`, `PERMIT NO.`, and the gateway-town return address.
2. Swap the URL / “Book a trip” line once the live domain is chosen.
3. Add **0.125 in bleed** if the printer asks for it (File → Print settings in Canva).
4. Keep the white **4.00 × 2.75 in** box on the back empty of photos.
5. Address line must read **LOCAL POSTAL CUSTOMER** (or Residential Customer).

## At the Post Office

- Use [USPS EDDM Retail](https://www.usps.com/business/every-door-direct-mail.htm) and Form 5110.
- Choose carrier routes in the gateway towns around the parks you want (Springdale, Bar Harbor, West Yellowstone, etc.).
- 2026 Retail postage is in the mid-$0.20s per piece; confirm at the counter.
